## Deferred intentionally

- Multi-provider live conversation is deferred until every response, memory and tool action can retain provider/model provenance. Development, research and script workers may participate now only through `docs/ORCHESTRATION.md` artifact handoffs.
- Emotion recognition is not treated as truth; only uncertain visible cues may enter context.
